Genesis email signatures

  1. Click Copy my signature under your name.
  2. In Gmail, click the gear icon → See all settings → scroll to Signature → Create new, name it "Genesis".
  3. Click in the signature box, press Ctrl+V (Mac: Cmd+V).
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick "Genesis" for new emails and replies/forwards.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.
